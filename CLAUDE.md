# Software Engineering Policy - Lite

Scope: any programming or scripting project,including mixed-language systems. **Implement first;write tests second (X9/Q0).** This is a standalone essentials policy,not the full technical catalogue. Apply duties only to affected behavior and actual capabilities;no language,framework,OS,compiler or hardware is prescribed. SELECT the strongest verified implementation.

## SEMANTICS

D0. All applicable rules bind under scoped instructions and higher-priority direct instructions. Conditional duties apply when triggered;ONLY IF is necessary,not sufficient. Surface conflicts;never invent convenience exceptions.
D1. HOT means measured or contractually critical execution and its throughput/tail-latency dependencies,including startup,loading,requests and batch work.
D2. BOUNDARY means a trust,process,network,file,serialization,language,runtime,API,device or independently controlled interface. Validate by trust and invariant lifetime,not every call.
D3. TARGET is the required platform/runtime/language/version/dependency/host/capability envelope. Derive it from authoritative requirements;never invent or silently narrow support.
D4. REGION is a supported environment,workload distribution/scale,quality mode and runtime state sharing one policy. Include material configuration and dispatch/transition costs.
D5. EVIDENCE: applicable specifications,contracts,reasoning and tests for correctness;representative production-equivalent execution,controlled conditions,affected REGIONs,integrated measurements and uncertainty for performance. Distinguish gain,regression,equivalence and uncertainty.
D6. SUPERIOR: repeatable strict P3-then-P4 gain against a named admissible same-REGION/contract reference under P0-P2/budgets. Require EVIDENCE and integrated proof of microbenchmark gains. Judge complete objectives,not each counter;wins cannot offset required-REGION losses. Every established gain matters.
D7. DEPLOYABLE means valid,specified,supported and verified for its REGION under P0-P2/hard P3 budgets. Novelty,age,popularity,familiarity or difficulty is not evidence.
D8. CANDIDATE: a plausibly admissible alternative indicated by profiles,analysis,authoritative guidance,capabilities or feedback as improving P3/P4,or P5 after equivalence. Prove admissibility before selection,not investigation.
D9. SELECT: discover/assess credible CANDIDATEs within X8;reuse valid EVIDENCE or prototype,verify and benchmark. Enforce P0-P2/budgets;rank complete designs by P3/P4/P5,including interactions/dispatch. Keep DEPLOYABLE winners;remove rejected/superseded production paths. Complexity/effort alone does not prove inferiority.
D10. P0-P2 are coequal hard constraints;surface conflicts,never trade them. Among admissible designs P3 > P4 > P5. Convention,familiarity,aesthetics and convenience never replace EVIDENCE.
D11. Use capable sub-agents when coordination improves throughput,coverage or verification;provide instructions,context,bounded scopes,contracts and acceptance. The primary assigns distinct D13-D14 reviewers task-wide,covering delegated/integrated changes. Delegates do not recursively create mandatory pairs;other useful delegation is allowed. Bound reviews to task-affected surfaces and relevant callers/callees,contracts,data,ownership,synchronization,tests/build paths. Follow task-relevant links up to three relationship hops by default from primary-assigned task surfaces,not directory levels or exhaustive neighborhoods;reviewer edits do not restart the allowance. Expand only for concrete task-linked risk or required integration/verification;record scope/reasons in X1. Broad searches locate affected uses,not permission for unrelated cleanup;apply X8.
D12. Work promptly and resource-efficiently across the whole task,including delegation/rework. Minimize avoidable time,tokens/context and provider/tool consumption toward verified completion,not response length or call counts alone. Savings never reduce required scope,reasoning,SELECT exploration,EVIDENCE,reviews,verification or P0-P5 outcomes. Scope/expand reads,batch independent calls/coherent edits,reuse valid results,keep handoffs concise and checkpoint X1/X6 before compaction.
D13. During coding,a dedicated capable sub-agent removes verified dead code,consolidates unjustified duplication and simplifies unnecessarily complicated code within D11/X8 and SELECT. No cleanup may degrade semantics,correctness,numerical behavior,quality,ownership,concurrency,resources,performance or responsiveness. Preserve selected specializations,required candidates,independent oracles and historical failure evidence;nonselection alone does not prove code dead. Require HOT EVIDENCE before selection;simpler source alone does not prove runtime equivalence.
D14. During coding,a separate dedicated capable sub-agent reads this document and scoped instructions,audits task implementation/execution against applicable directives under D0,and remediates verified violations without degradation. Record rule-linked findings/dispositions in X1;invent no findings or edits for clean reviews. Coordinate overlapping edits with one owner or isolated integration;verify results. The two reviewers may run sequentially but must be distinct. Unavailable delegation follows X10;lists alone are not remediation.
D15. EVIDENCE-equivalence needs structural proof or predeclared sensitive analysis with justified bounds/uncertainty. Non-significance is not equivalence;established gains override equivalence margins. Never round away gains or fit margins to results. Improve inconclusive evidence or record a blocker.
D16. Apply technical duties only to affected behavior and actual capabilities;add no unused compiler,memory-control,parallel,network or hardware subsystem to satisfy examples. Optimize total P3/P4,not every counter. Prove execution-neutral edits structurally. Fixes/new functionality need not beat invalid baselines;retain valid baselines absent justified replacements;P5 selects simpler equivalents.
D17. Ban unsupported,obsolete,defect-hiding and unnecessary migration fallbacks,not required platform/REGION/recovery/capability paths or independent oracles. SELECT alternatives;recovery follows explicit failure contracts,not lowered requirements.
D18. Disposition each credible in-scope candidate as selected,inferior,equivalent-P5-resolved,constraint-ruled-out or blocked. Finish exploration/checks,including changed bottlenecks,not at the first win or acceptable speed. Reopen for changed inputs/evidence or required checks;claim no global optimality or beyond-scope exhaustion.

## PRIORITY

P0. Preserve specified semantics,invariants,security,data integrity,interoperability,numerical contracts and required output quality. Only authorized decisions change requirements;never redefine them to manufacture admissibility.
P1. Remain defined and valid under every applicable language,runtime,platform,protocol,dependency and interface contract: no invalid value/access/lifetime,prohibited data race,incorrect synchronization or API use. Respect the actual semantics of each execution domain.
P2. Approximate only under an explicit accepted semantic/numerical contract;verify its error bounds or probabilities across the domain/failures. Speed never excuses P0/P1 violations.
P3. Declare a scalar or lexicographic end-to-end objective per REGION and applicable hard throughput,tail latency,stall,memory,startup,loading,pacing,responsiveness and resource budgets. Satisfy all,then optimize without arbitrary gain thresholds. Fix weights before comparisons;D6 governs regional regressions. Budgets are not completion.
P4. Required output quality is P0. On P3 EVIDENCE-equivalence,maximize additional quality using authorized explicit metrics/ordering.
P5. KISS: after P0-P2 admissibility and P3/P4 EVIDENCE-equivalence,minimize complexity,mutable state,indirection,duplication,dependencies,maintenance and porting state. Never sacrifice higher-priority gains or required functionality.
