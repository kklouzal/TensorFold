onecat_dmv13_core.cuh is byte-identical to upstream
csrc/sm70_turbomind/ops/gguf_dmv13_core.cuh (Git blob
30d368ccc7f5cb3c94972494fb5130af524511ab).
onecat_moe_gu.cu derives its expert-grouped kernel from upstream
csrc/sm70_turbomind/ops/gguf_moe_gu_sm70.cu (Git blob
a4a83d36dfe790956f427d517e7b7d0f2ca046f2).
TensorFold modifications: E256 global-ID remapping; distinct-ID validation;
independent shared/foreign slots; C1..32 via bounded8-token panels; no Q8_1;
separate original1Cat earlyHalf and TensorFold FP32 activation epilogues;
owned typed/extent/alias/device boundaries and startup native preparation.
onecat_moe_packing.py adapts upstream gguf_moe_planes.py (Git blob
a799ab3425af40527ed289d5535bb01da09c9ff4) and gguf_dmv_formats.py
(Git blob fd0580f8ffc1311ad43640836efc0f7a577732de), with owned E256/lane160
validation, bounded expert staging, existing project codebooks and IQ4_XS
plane preparation. The remaining wrappers/tests are TensorFold-owned.
onecat_gpu_packing.cu implements that pinned GU plane representation for
active E256 experts with unique writers and caller-owned GPU frame storage;
the original CPU producer is retained as an independent wire-layout oracle.
onecat_moe_down.cu adapts upstream gguf_dp4a_sm70.cu, gguf_q8_1.cuh and
gguf_dp4a.cuh at the same pinned revision for explicit Q8_1/integer-dot
methods, owned E256 routing, bounded scratch and separate reduction contracts.
Additional TensorFold modifications (2026-11-12): unweighted Half/Float down
slots preserving the caller's shared-expert and weighting boundary; bounded
target/prefill panels; direct raw-IQ2 gate/up; and a separately prepared
prefill union pack with one writer per expert. MTP keeps its original provider.
The integer-dot formulas and Q8_1 layout follow GGML/llama.cpp (MIT);
the complete notice is retained in LICENSES/GGML-MIT.txt.
